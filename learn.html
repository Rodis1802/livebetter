LiveBetter Guide to a Healthier Life

Nutrition

A balanced diet includes fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats. Reducing processed foods, added sugars, and excessive salt intake lowers the risk of chronic diseases and supports energy and mood.

Exercise

Regular physical activity improves cardiovascular health, strengthens muscles, and enhances mental well-being. Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week, and include strength training twice weekly.

Sleep

Sleep is essential for recovery and cognitive performance. Adults should aim for 7–9 hours per night. Consistent sleep schedules, a quiet environment, and limited screen time before bed help improve sleep quality.

Mental Well-being

Managing stress through mindfulness, social connection, and physical activity contributes to overall well-being. Practices like meditation, journaling, or seeking support when needed are valuable for emotional health.

Healthy Habits

Staying hydrated, avoiding smoking and excessive alcohol, and scheduling regular health check-ups are key practices. Building routines around these principles supports a sustainable, healthy lifestyle over time.

Trusted Sources

Our guidance is based on recommendations from organizations like the World Health Organization (WHO), MedlinePlus (U.S. National Library of Medicine), and the National Institute of Health (NIH).
